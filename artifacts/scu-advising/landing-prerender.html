Pre-pilot · Santa Clara University planning

Plan your degree and each quarter in one place.

CampusVal helps organize and compare planning information from SCU sources. Workday and SCU remain authoritative for official academic records and decisions.

Available only to invited pilot participants while SCU reviews hosting, security, and rollout.

  • Degree Planning
    Build and organize a long-term academic plan from published requirements.
  • Quarter Planning
    Arrange a quarter schedule and review it before registering in Workday.
  • Explore Options with Tentative Degree Planning
    Explore different majors, minors, and academic pathways without changing your declared SCU program.
CampusVal is a pre-pilot planning tool under review. It is not an official SCU system and does not register, declare, or certify anything.
